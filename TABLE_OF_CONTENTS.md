## Continuity audit prompts
- Clarify measurement, observed patterns, statistical inference, and later model evaluation.
- Connect preserving knowledge to information without implying storage preserves meaning.
- Bridge representation and encoding to systematic reasoning and computation.
- Make the progression from transactions and analytical workloads to scale, distributed reliability, cloud, platforms, and organisational trust clear.
- Distinguish uncertainty, causality, prediction, and learning from examples.
- Explain AI’s evolution without implying newer approaches universally replace older ones.
- Connect generation, grounding, and evaluation; clarify what outputs do and do not establish.
- Separate decision support, decision quality, automated action, oversight, and learning from outcomes.
- Audit whether security, privacy, and governance receive sufficient attention across the book.
- Use systems thinking throughout; Chapter 38 should synthesise it, not introduce it for the first time.

The chapter list is a hypothesis. Keep, split, merge, reorder, or remove chapters when there is a clear, reader-centred reason.
