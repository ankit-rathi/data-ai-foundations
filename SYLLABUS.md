## Important syllabus principle

The syllabus defines the learning journey, not a fixed amount of content.

- Some chapters will require more capsules than others.
- Some concepts will be explained only to supporting depth.
- Some concepts will deserve deeper exploration.
- Chapters may evolve as gaps or overlaps become apparent.
- No capsule count or page count should dictate completeness.

The objective is coherent understanding, not maximum content volume.
